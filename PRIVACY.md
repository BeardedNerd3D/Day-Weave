# Day Weave Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 17, 2026

Day Weave is designed with privacy as a core product principle. The app helps individuals and households prioritize tasks, coordinate shared responsibilities, and understand what matters most while minimizing unnecessary data collection and external processing.

## Overview

Day Weave is designed to:

- Store app data locally on your device.
- Use your personal iCloud account for supported synchronization and household sharing.
- Keep personal information separate from household-shared information.
- Avoid selling, renting, or monetizing personal task or household data.
- Avoid third-party advertising and tracking.
- Avoid requiring a third-party AI account or API key.
- Use Apple technologies for supported intelligence, synchronization, purchases, and calendar export.

## Data Stored by Day Weave

Depending on how you use the app, Day Weave may store information such as:

- Personal tasks
- Shared household tasks
- Due dates
- Scheduled dates
- Estimated task durations
- Task notes
- Priority preferences
- Priority feedback
- Household memberships
- Household task assignments
- Assignment acceptance or decline state
- Household priority perspectives
- Priority-resolution information
- Task history
- Completion status
- App settings

This information is used only to provide Day Weave functionality.

## Personal and Household Data

### Personal Data

Personal data may include personal tasks, private notes, personal priority preferences, task history, recommendation feedback, and ranking information.

Joining a Day Weave household does not automatically make personal tasks or personal priority information visible to other household members.

### Household-Shared Data

Information intentionally created or shared within a Day Weave household may be visible to other members of that household.

Shared information may include household tasks, due dates, scheduled dates, assignments, assignment status, member priority perspectives, resolved household priorities, and completion activity.

## iCloud and CloudKit

Day Weave uses Apple's CloudKit technology for supported synchronization and household sharing.

CloudKit data is associated with your Apple Account and handled through Apple's iCloud infrastructure.

Day Weave does not operate a separate cloud database that independently stores copies of your task and household data.

## Apple Intelligence and Foundation Models

On supported devices, Day Weave may use Apple's Foundation Models framework to enhance natural-language task interpretation, context extraction, recommendation explanations, and priority-conflict summaries.

Day Weave's core priority engine does not require a third-party AI service.

The app does not require an OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or other third-party AI API key.

The deterministic priority engine remains functional even when Apple Intelligence is unavailable.

## Apple Calendar

Day Weave may provide an optional **Add to Apple Calendar** action.

When selected, Day Weave may use Apple's EventKit or EventKitUI frameworks to help create an Apple Calendar event.

Day Weave does not require access to your full calendar simply to maintain its own task calendar.

Apple Calendar integration is user initiated.

## Purchases

Day Weave may offer **Day Weave Unlimited** as a one-time non-consumable in-app purchase.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit.

Day Weave does not receive or store your full payment-card information.

Where supported, the Day Weave Unlimited entitlement may be shared through Apple Family Sharing.

Apple Family Sharing of a purchase is separate from Day Weave household-data sharing.

## Analytics and Tracking

Day Weave does not use your task or household data for advertising.

Day Weave does not sell your personal data.

Day Weave does not use third-party advertising SDKs.

If diagnostic or crash information is made available through Apple's developer tools, it may be used only to improve app stability and quality.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Day Weave data remains on your device and, when applicable, in your iCloud account until it is deleted through the app, removed through supported household-management workflows, or otherwise removed through Apple-provided account and storage controls.

Deleting shared household information may affect other household members who have access to that information.

## Children's Privacy

Day Weave is intended as a general productivity and household-coordination application and is not designed as a child-directed service.

## Security

Day Weave relies on Apple's platform security, local device protections, iCloud infrastructure, CloudKit permissions, and App Store purchase systems.

No software system can guarantee absolute security, but Day Weave is designed to minimize unnecessary data collection and external data exposure.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Day Weave evolves.

Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this document.

## Contact

For privacy questions or support, use the contact information provided on the Day Weave support page or App Store listing.
