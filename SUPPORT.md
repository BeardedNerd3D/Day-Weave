# Day Weave Support

Welcome to Day Weave support.

Day Weave helps you prioritize what matters, coordinate shared household responsibilities, delegate tasks, and understand what can safely wait.

## What is Day Weave?

Day Weave is a personal and household prioritization app built around four main ideas:

- **My Day** — what matters most to you today
- **Our Day** — what matters most to your household
- **Not Today** — tasks that can safely wait
- **Why This Matters** — an explanation of why something was prioritized

Day Weave is not intended to replace Apple Calendar or become a full project-management system.

## Personal Tasks

You can create personal tasks with information such as title, notes, due date, scheduled date, estimated duration, priority information, and completion status.

Personal tasks remain separate from household-shared tasks unless you intentionally place them into a shared household context.

## Shared Household Tasks

Household members can coordinate shared responsibilities using shared tasks with due dates, priority perspectives, assignments, assignment status, and completion state.

## My Day

My Day is your personal prioritized view.

A shared task may appear differently in each member's My Day because responsibility, assignment state, deadlines, and personal priorities may differ.

## Our Day

Our Day is the shared household view. It can surface important household tasks, assigned responsibilities, unassigned work, priority disagreements, completed shared tasks, and tasks that can wait.

## Priority Differences

Different household members do not need to rank a task the same way.

For example:

- One member may view a task as Priority 1.
- Another may view it as Priority 3.
- Day Weave may resolve the household recommendation to Priority 2.

Day Weave does not simply average every disagreement. Deadlines, responsibility, consequences, dependencies, and other structured factors may affect the final recommendation.

## Delegation

Day Weave Unlimited supports household task delegation.

Assignments may be:

- Assigned
- Accepted
- Declined
- Reassigned
- Completed

Assigning a task does not automatically mean the recipient has accepted responsibility.

## Day Weave Unlimited

Day Weave's personal prioritization experience is free.

**Day Weave Unlimited** is a one-time purchase that unlocks collaborative household intelligence, including:

- Independent household priority perspectives
- Household priority resolution
- Priority mismatch detection
- Delegation
- Assignment acceptance and decline
- Reassignment
- Personalized household task ranking
- Our Day collaborative prioritization

There is no subscription required for Day Weave Unlimited.

## Family Sharing

Where supported by Apple, Day Weave Unlimited may be shared using Apple Family Sharing.

Family Sharing shares the purchase entitlement. It does not automatically create a Day Weave household, invite household members, or share Day Weave task data.

## Apple Intelligence

On supported devices, Day Weave may use Apple's Foundation Models to improve natural-language interpretation and explanations.

The core Day Weave priority engine remains deterministic and does not require Apple Intelligence to function.

## Apple Calendar

Day Weave includes its own lightweight task calendar.

If you want an individual task represented in Apple Calendar, use the optional **Add to Apple Calendar** action where available.

## iCloud Sync

Day Weave uses Apple's CloudKit technology for supported synchronization and household sharing.

If synchronization appears delayed:

1. Confirm you are signed in to iCloud.
2. Confirm the device has an internet connection.
3. Confirm any household invitation was accepted.
4. Reopen Day Weave.
5. Allow CloudKit time to synchronize.

Offline changes may synchronize after connectivity returns.

## Troubleshooting Purchases

If Day Weave Unlimited is not recognized:

1. Confirm you are signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase.
2. Open Day Weave settings.
3. Use **Restore Purchases** if available.
4. Confirm App Store services are available.
5. If using Family Sharing, confirm the eligible purchase is available to the family member.

## Reporting a Problem

Please include:

- Day Weave version
- Build number
- Device model
- iOS or iPadOS version
- A short description of what happened
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Whether the problem involves tasks, household sharing, purchases, Apple Intelligence, or Apple Calendar

Please avoid including sensitive personal task content unless necessary.
